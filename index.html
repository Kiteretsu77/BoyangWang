Boyang Wang

I'm Boyang Wang (王博洋), and I work on video generation. I believe visual context compression, especially for video, is a key source of intelligence for video models.

I'm currently a second-year CS PhD student at the University of Virginia (UVA), supervised by Zezhou Cheng.

Before joining UVA, I earned an M.S. in Robotics and a B.S. in Computer Science from the University of Michigan. At UMich, I worked with Prof. JJ Park, Prof. Nima Fazeli, Prof. Hun-Seok Kim, and Prof. Chad Jenkins.

Since the summer of 2026, I have been interning at Adobe, where I am fortunate to be mentored by Matheus Gadelha. During the summer of 2025, I interned at Shanghai AI Lab, working closely with Xudong Xu in the Embodied AI Department.

My research interests mainly lie in Generative Models (especially video) for General, Robotics, and Anime domains!

I am actively seeking research internship opportunities for Summer 2027 in generative models, especially video generation. Feel free to reach out!
